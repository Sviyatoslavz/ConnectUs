# Finance, banking, insurance

## What matters
Compliance, trust, and approval flow. The buyer's personal risk of being blamed for a bad
choice usually outweighs the upside of a good one — so reduce their risk visibly.

## Discovery questions
- What regulatory or internal requirements does the solution have to satisfy?
- What's the approval chain, and how long has a comparable decision taken?
- What happens if this fails an audit?
- Who has veto power?

## Approach
Lead with references from regulated peers, documentation, and audit trail.
Never oversell — a claim that fails compliance review kills the deal permanently.
Expect long cycles; measure progress in stages cleared, not enthusiasm.

## Objections
- "Нужно согласование" → map every approver by name and prepare a one-page internal document
  for your champion to circulate.
- "Риски" → quantify them and present the mitigations before they ask.

## KPI
Cycle length by stage, approval pass rate, compliance rejection rate, LTV, retention,
cross-sell ratio, cost of acquisition per approved client.
