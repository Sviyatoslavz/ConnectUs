# Real estate and construction

## What matters
Trust and risk reduction. The purchase is large, infrequent, emotional, and involves several
people who each fear a different thing.

## Discovery questions
- What are you solving with this purchase — for whom, and by when?
- What would make this the wrong decision?
- How is it financed, and is that confirmed?
- Who else is involved in the decision, and what matters most to them?
- What have you already looked at, and why didn't it fit?

## Value levers
Documented legal cleanliness, verifiable timelines, references from completed projects,
staged payments, transparent change-order pricing, and a named person responsible.

## Objections
- "Дорого" → compare per square metre against the alternatives they actually saw, and include
  finishing, timeline risk, and cost of delay.
- "Подумаем" → almost always a second decision maker not in the room. Ask who else must agree.
- "Сроки" → give a schedule with milestones and penalties. Vague timelines read as risk.

## KPI
Showings-to-offer, offer-to-contract, average days on market, referral share,
cost per qualified lead, change-order margin.
