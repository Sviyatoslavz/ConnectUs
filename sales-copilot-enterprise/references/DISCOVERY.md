# Discovery and positioning

## SPIN — the question sequence

| Type | Purpose | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Situation** | Background context | Keep to a minimum — research it beforehand instead of asking |
| **Problem** | Specific friction, dissatisfaction, failures | This is where most reps stop |
| **Implication** | What the problem costs over time, in money and consequence | This is where deals are actually made |
| **Need-payoff** | Get the buyer to state the value of solving it | The buyer must say it, not you |

Implication questions to keep ready:
> "Во что это обходится за год?"
> "Сколько клиентов вы из-за этого теряете?"
> "Что произойдёт, если оставить как есть ещё полгода?"
> "Кто ещё внутри компании страдает от этого?"

Need-payoff:
> "Если бы это решилось, что изменилось бы для вас лично?"
> "Насколько это важно по сравнению с другими вашими задачами?"

## GAP selling

- **Current state** — processes, costs, workarounds, what breaks and how often
- **Future state** — the metric they want, and by when
- **The gap** — quantified. The gap is the reason to buy; your product is only the bridge.

If you cannot state the gap in a number, discovery isn't finished. Go back before proposing.

## Other discovery frames

- **NEAT** — Need, Economic impact, Access to authority, Timeline. Compact, good for mid-market.
- **SPICED** — Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical event, Decision process. The critical event is the
  urgency driver; without one, the deal will slip regardless of interest.

Always hunt for the critical event: contract expiry, season, audit, regulation, a competitor's move,
a hiring plan, a deadline someone is personally accountable for. Deals without one don't close on time.

## Challenger — breaking status quo

Use when the client is comfortable and sees no reason to change. Sequence:

1. **Warm-up** — show you understand their world; earn the right to challenge
2. **Reframe** — one non-obvious insight that changes how they see their own problem
3. **Rational drowning** — the numbers behind the cost of inaction
4. **Emotional impact** — connect it to what this specific person is measured on
5. **Value proposition** — the capabilities required to solve it, stated vendor-neutrally
6. **Our solution** — only now, and only mapped to those capabilities

The reframe is the whole method. Without a genuine insight, this becomes an aggressive pitch and
damages trust. If you don't have a real insight for that industry, use consultative discovery instead.

## Discovery before demo

Never demonstrate before quantified pain exists. A demo given too early converts a buyer into a
feature evaluator, and feature comparisons are won on price.
