# Proposals and commercial copy

## Proposal structure

A proposal is a decision document, not a company brochure. One page for SMB, three maximum
for enterprise.

1. **Their situation** — in their words, from the discovery call. This is the most important
   section: it proves you listened, and it's what gets forwarded internally.
2. **The result** — what changes, with numbers and a timeframe.
3. **What's included** — concrete deliverables, not capabilities.
4. **Price** — clear, with options if useful. Never hidden or "on request".
5. **Timeline** — start date, milestones, when they see the first result.
6. **Why us** — two or three lines maximum, tied to their case, with proof.
7. **Next step** — one action, with a date.

Do not open with company history. Nobody reads it, and it signals that the proposal is generic.

## Pricing presentation

- Three options works well: reduced / recommended / extended. Most buyers take the middle.
- Anchor high first, then present the recommended option — the middle looks reasonable by contrast.
- Present the price in the unit that makes it comparable to their existing costs
  (per month, per client, per shipment) — but always show the total too.
- Never discount without a trade: longer term, prepayment, larger scope, a case study, a referral.

## Follow-up emails

Subject lines: specific and short. "Расчёт по [конкретика]" beats "Коммерческое предложение".

Body rules: one idea, under 120 words, one question, one call to action. If it needs scrolling
on a phone, it's too long.

## Cold email

1. First line about them — a specific, verifiable detail. Generic openers get deleted.
2. Second line: the result you produced for someone like them, with a number.
3. Third line: one low-friction question.
4. No attachments, no links in the first email.

Under 80 words. The goal is a reply, not a sale.

## Closing message

When a deal has stalled after several follow-ups, send a clean close rather than another nudge:

> "[Имя], похоже, сейчас это не в приоритете — это нормально.
> Закрываю задачу с моей стороны, чтобы не отвлекать.
> Если ситуация изменится — напишите, вернёмся к этому за один день."

This recovers a surprising number of deals, and frees time on the ones it doesn't.

## Copy principles

- The next step must be obvious without re-reading.
- Every claim gets a number or a name attached, or it gets cut.
- Write in the client's language, matching their level of formality.
- Cut every sentence that would survive being pasted into a competitor's proposal unchanged.
