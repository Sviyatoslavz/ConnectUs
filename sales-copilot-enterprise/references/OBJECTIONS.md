# Objection handling

## Method — LAER

1. **Listen** — let them finish completely. Interrupting an objection guarantees you'll answer the
   wrong one.
2. **Acknowledge** — without agreeing. "Понимаю, это важный вопрос" costs nothing and lowers defenses.
   Never argue, never say "но".
3. **Explore** — this is the step almost everyone skips, and it decides the outcome. Ask what's
   behind the words before answering. Then isolate:
   > "Если бы этот вопрос решился, что-то ещё мешало бы начать?"
   This separates the real blocker from the decoy.
4. **Respond** — answer the *explored* objection, not the stated one, then move toward commitment.
   Every response ends with a question or a proposed next step. Never end on a defense.

Responding before exploring is the single most common and most expensive mistake in sales
conversations: you end up defending against an objection the buyer didn't actually have.

Never: discount immediately, over-explain, or send more materials as a substitute for a conversation.

## Catalogue

### "Дорого"
Real meaning: value not established, or comparing against a different thing, or no budget authority.
Response: separate price from cost. Ask what they're comparing to. Quantify the cost of the current
situation over a year. Offer scope reduction before price reduction.
> "Дорого по сравнению с чем? Давайте посчитаем, во что вам обходится текущая ситуация за год —
> и сравним с этой цифрой."

### "Нет времени"
Real meaning: not a priority yet. Time always exists for priorities.
Response: don't fight for more time — shrink the ask. Offer a 10-minute version with a specific
outcome attached.
> "Тогда не буду занимать много. Десять минут во вторник — и вы получите готовый расчёт по вашему
> случаю. Если не будет полезно, больше не побеспокою."

### "Нет бюджета"
Real meaning: no budget *for this*, or wrong person, or the budget cycle hasn't opened.
Response: find out when the budget opens and who owns it. Ask what it would take to be funded.
Keep the relationship warm with a scheduled date, not "I'll ping you sometime".

### "У нас уже есть поставщик"
Real meaning: switching feels risky. Not the same as satisfaction.
Response: don't attack the incumbent. Ask what they'd improve if they could change one thing.
Position as a backup or a small parallel test, not a replacement.
> "Логично, вы бы и не искали. Просто чтобы вы знали, к кому обратиться, если что-то пойдёт не так.
> Что бы вы у них поменяли, если бы могли поменять одно?"

### "Отправьте информацию"
Real meaning: polite exit, in most cases.
Response: agree, but attach a condition and a date. Never send and wait silently.
> "Отправлю. Чтобы не слать общую презентацию — что именно вам важно увидеть? И давайте сразу
> договоримся: в четверг в 11 созвонимся на 10 минут по вашим вопросам."

### "Я подумаю"
Real meaning: an unspoken concern, or no authority to decide.
Response: name it kindly and give permission to say no.
> "Конечно. Обычно 'подумаю' означает, что что-то не сходится — цена, сроки или нужно с кем-то
> согласовать. Что из этого ближе?"

### "Не интересно"
Real meaning: the opening was about you, not about them.
Response: don't push. Ask one question tied to their business result and let them close the door.
Then leave a door open with a date.

### "Не сейчас, позже"
Real meaning: no urgency exists.
Response: find the external driver — season, contract expiry, a competitor's move, a deadline.
If there's genuinely no driver, get a specific date and put it in the calendar. "Позже" without
a date means lost.

### "Надо согласовать"
Real meaning: you're not talking to the decision maker.
Response: ask to be present at the approval conversation, or arm your contact with a one-page
internal summary. Never let someone else present your value for you without materials.
> "Кто ещё участвует в решении? Могу подготовить короткое резюме, чтобы вам было проще
> объяснить — или присоединиться на 10 минут, чтобы ответить на технические вопросы."

### "Сравниваем варианты"
Response: help them compare properly. Give an honest criteria list — including where competitors
are stronger. Credibility from honesty beats a one-sided comparison.

### "Изменились приоритеты"
Response: usually true and usually temporary. Establish when the topic returns, who will own it
then, and schedule that conversation now.

## Enterprise variants

### "У конкурента дешевле"
Explore first: are they comparing entry price or total cost of ownership — implementation,
customization, downtime, maintenance, internal hours? Most comparisons collapse once the full
picture is on the table. Then give an honest differentiator with a number attached, and concede
openly where the competitor is genuinely stronger. Selective honesty buys credibility on everything else.

### "Нас устраивает текущий поставщик"
Never attack the incumbent — it insults the person who chose them. Explore instead:
> "Логично. Гипотетически: если бы один показатель в текущем процессе можно было улучшить —
> какой это был бы?"
Then position as a parallel test or a backup, not a replacement. Switching risk is the real objection.

### "Нужно согласование с закупками / юристами / безопасностью"
Not an objection — a process step you failed to map. Get the steps, the owners, and the typical
duration of each. Offer to supply documentation before it's requested.

### "Вернёмся к этому в следующем квартале"
Explore whether the budget cycle is the real driver or a polite exit. If real, find the critical
event that makes the timeline move, and schedule the conversation that precedes budget planning —
not the one after it, when the money is already allocated elsewhere.

## Prevention beats handling

Most objections are symptoms of a weak earlier stage. If price objections dominate — the value
work in discovery is missing. If "I'll think about it" dominates — no urgency and no decision
maker. Fix the stage, not the rebuttal.
