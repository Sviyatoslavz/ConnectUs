# Scripts

Scripts are skeletons, not text to read aloud. Write them so a rep can improvise inside the
structure. Always adapt wording to the client's language and the seller's actual voice.

## Cold call structure

1. **Identify + reason for calling** (10 sec) — name, company, one sentence on why *them* specifically.
2. **Permission** — "Удобно 30 секунд?" Respect the answer.
3. **Hook** — a result achieved for a similar business, with a number.
4. **One question** — about their current situation, not about your product.
5. **Ask for the next step**, not for the sale. A specific date and time.

> "Добрый день, [имя]. Меня зовут [имя], компания [X]. Звоню, потому что видел [конкретная деталь
> о них]. Удобно 30 секунд?
> Мы делаем [результат] для [похожий бизнес] — у них [конкретная цифра].
> Скажите, как вы сейчас решаете [задача]?
> Предлагаю встретиться на 15 минут — вторник в 11 или среда в 15?"

## First meeting / discovery

Talk for 30% of the time, maximum. The rep who talks less closes more.

Question order:
1. Situation — how it works now
2. Problem — what doesn't work
3. Consequence — what it costs them (money, time, clients, nerves)
4. Value — what changes if it's solved
5. Process — who decides, by when, what the approval path is

The consequence questions are where deals are made. Most reps skip them.

> "Сколько клиентов в месяц вы теряете из-за этого?"
> "Во что это обходится за год?"
> "Что произойдёт, если оставить как есть ещё полгода?"

## Field-sales opening (in person)

The owner is busy and mid-task. You have about 15 seconds before they decide.

1. Greet, name the business specifically — proves you're not doing a mass round
2. One sentence: what you do, in the outcome the owner cares about
3. Show something physical or on screen — 10 seconds of demo beats 2 minutes of description
4. One question about their situation
5. Concrete next step, closed with a date

Rules: never come at peak hours; ask for the owner by role if not by name; if they're busy,
ask when to come back and *come back exactly then* — that alone puts you ahead of most competitors.

## Demo

Show only what solves the pain they named. Every feature you show that they didn't ask about
lowers the perceived relevance of the ones they did.

Structure: recap their problem in their words → show the one thing that fixes it → ask
"насколько это близко к тому, что вам нужно?" → handle what comes back → propose next step.

## Closing

Ask directly. Softening the close is the most common reason for a lost deal.

- Direct: "Давайте начнём. Оформляю?"
- Alternative: "Начнём с базового варианта или сразу с расширенного?"
- Next-step: "Если всё подходит — что нужно с вашей стороны, чтобы стартовать на следующей неделе?"
- Summary: recap agreed value, then ask.

After asking — stay silent. The first person to speak concedes.

## Follow-up

Every follow-up carries something new: a case, a number, an answer to their question, a deadline.
Never "просто напоминаю о себе" — it teaches the client to ignore you.

Cadence for a warm deal: day 1 (recap + materials), day 3 (answer to open question), day 7 (case
from a similar business), day 14 (direct question about status), day 30 (close or park explicitly).

> "[Имя], добрый день. Обещал прислать расчёт по [конкретика] — он в приложении.
> Ключевое: [одна цифра].
> Готовы стартовать до конца месяца? Если сроки сдвинулись — скажите, когда вернуться."
